Trends over time

Calculated episode = consecutive samples above the threshold; a gap ends the episode. This is not a clinical event reported by the PAP device. Days without data ≠ days without use.

Glasgow Index · experimental

Flow-shape analysis, calculated locally from 25 Hz flow. Not an RDI, RERA detector or diagnosis. Distinct from ResMed flow limitation.

Method and attribution

Nine-component overall GI (including Top Heavy), matching the reference multi-night analyzer. Component fractions rounded per session and duration-weighted per night. Sessions and missing samples are not joined across gaps. Unanalyzable runs are excluded and analyzed duration is shown. Short nights are retained unless you hide them. Trends do not imply a causal effect of pressure.

Algorithm: DaveSkvn / GlasgowIndex; multi-night reference: VibeCoder75321 / Multi-Night-Glasgow-Index-Analyzer. GPL-3.0-or-later. See README and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the source distribution.
