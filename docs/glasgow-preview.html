How to read these trends
Mean GI is the nightly nine-component Glasgow Index (0–9). The optional trailing mean uses calendar days and excludes unavailable values. Missing measurements have no bar. Calculated snore/flow-limitation episodes are consecutive samples above the chosen threshold, not device-reported clinical events. GI describes flow shape and is not an RDI or a diagnosis.
